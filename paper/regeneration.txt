How to regenerate every number in the paper
  "Does coupling divergence tell a god unit from a complex algorithm?"

Every number the paper prints is produced by one script, paper/scripts/tables.py (in the harness
repository, https://github.com/officefloor/concentration-divergence-study; not part of this
ancillary bundle), from the harness's confirmatory analysis file (anc/analysis/analysis.json),
the set-aside first analysis (anc/analysis/analysis.v1.json), the explore-half decisions
(anc/decisions/), each set's validation record (anc/<set>/validation.json), the MLCQ rows
(anc/mlcq/study.json), the label and sample summaries, and analysis-hashes.txt. No number the
study computes is typed by hand (literature constants and the plan's own constants are the
exceptions): main.tex quotes macros from generated/numbers.tex and includes tables from
generated/tab_*.tex, both written by that script. If the script's output ever disagrees with
the prose, the script is the authority.

ENVIRONMENT
  The harness's virtualenv (../.venv, from ../step1_setup.sh) runs the script; it imports the
  harness only for the optional count of the reviewed units the human populations leave out
  (Section 6), which needs results/<set>/scores/*.npz. Without them the script still writes
  every other macro and prints a note. pdflatex (TeX Live) builds the PDF.

ALL AT ONCE (from the repository root)
  .venv/bin/python paper/scripts/tables.py     # generated/numbers.tex and generated/tab_*.tex
  cd paper && ./build.sh                       # main.pdf, abstract.txt, arxiv.tar.gz

TABLES (generated/), in the order the paper places them
  Table 1  tab_primary               primary/joined: arms, decomposition, size_null,
                                     with_constructors; secondary/joined/other_arms (X*.O_flow)
  Table 2  tab_leaf                  leaf/joined; secondary/joined/method/leaf;
                                     secondary/joined/fontana/leaf
  Table 3  tab_cells_human           consistency/joined/cells, human oracles, flow
  Table 4  tab_cells_tool            consistency/joined/cells, tool and internal oracles, flow
  Table 5  tab_other_arms            secondary/joined/other_arms
  Table 6  tab_method                secondary/joined/method
  Table 7  tab_tool_oracles          secondary/joined/tool_oracles (+ _with_constructors)
  Table 8  tab_friedman              secondary/joined/matrix/<oracle>/friedman, flow
  Table 9  tab_intersection          exploratory_intersection/joined
  Also written, not placed: tab_xstar_class, tab_xstar_method (decisions/explore-choices.json),
  tab_between_definitions (consistency/joined/between_definitions).

INLINE NUMBERS (generated/numbers.tex, about 800 macros)
  prim*        primary/joined and primary/javaparser (micro, macro, arms, AUC, gate, verdict,
               decomposition, size null, with constructors, mechanism)
  leaf*        leaf/joined (flow, non-private) and the method and Fontana leaf diagnostics
  cons*, pOne*, pTwo*, pThree*, pFour*, cell*, cells*, between*
               consistency/joined (predictions, verdict, cells, between definitions) and
               consistency/javaparser
  sec*, rho*, tertile*, meth*, tool*, fontana*, fried*, matrix*
               secondary/joined (other_arms, independence, tertiles, method, tool_oracles,
               fontana, matrix) and the JavaParser-set counterparts named *Jp*
  sens*        sensitivity/joined and sensitivity/javaparser
  inter*       exploratory_intersection/joined
  join*, calls*, role*, throwNew*, di*, pruned*, files*, scale*, zero*, uncoupled*, labels*,
  agree*, pmdCog*, mlcq*, seedThree*, fontana*, validation*
               diagnostics/<set>, results/<set>/validation.json,
               decisions/measurer-validation.mlcq-explore.json, labels/mlcq-summary.json,
               sample/seed3-measured.json, results/mlcq/study.json
  xClass*, xMethod*, gate*
               decisions/explore-choices.json
  *VOne*       results/analysis.v1.json (how the set-aside analysis differs)
  left*        the reviewed units the human populations leave out, from the score files
               through core.Population (Section 6)

CONSTANTS TYPED BY HAND
  The outlier rule's 3.5, the gate's target half-width 0.10 and floor 20, the bootstrap's 2000
  and 12345 (also read from the analysis file), the literature's 0.51 and 0.81 (Madeyski and
  Lewowski 2023), the method count's 47 (WMC), Fontana's 420 and 140 instances, and the
  measurer's 8-hour budget and 9 GB heap.
